# Scentify Onboarding and Authentication Redesign

## Direction

Unify both screens in a warm editorial-luxury system: ivory canvas, espresso type, restrained antique-gold accents, soft botanical fragrance imagery, serif display headings, and clean sans-serif controls.

## Onboarding

- Keep the Scentify wordmark, Skip action, existing headline, supporting sentence, and Get started CTA.
- Replace the oversized framed illustration with a full-bleed editorial perfume composition that fades naturally into the ivory page.
- Keep one clear primary action and generous negative space.

## Authentication

- Replace the disconnected black treatment with the same ivory editorial system.
- Keep email, password with visibility control, Forgot password, Log in, Google, Create account, Terms of Use, and Privacy Policy actions.
- Remove phone authentication from this screen.
- Use a small perfume still-life or botanical crop as atmosphere, without competing with the form.

## Constraints

- Portrait mobile layout at the supplied screen proportions.
- Accessible contrast and touch-sized controls.
- No marketplace styling, extra features, glassmorphism, gradients, or ornamental clutter.
- These are image drafts only; Flutter implementation is out of scope.
